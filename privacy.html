Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Data we access

When the account owner authorizes Past Whispered Uploader, TikTok provides an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID, and basic profile information (display name and username), plus the permissions granted (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

2. How it is used

This data is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account and to show which account is being used. No analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's personal computer. There is no server and no database. Nothing is sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the local token files at any time and revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

6. Children

The Tool is not directed to children and has no users other than its adult owner.

7. Contact

Questions can be sent via The Past Whispered channel page.
