Past Whispered Uploader

Past Whispered Uploader is a private desktop tool used only by the creator of The Past Whispered, a channel of short history documentaries in Indonesian.

The tool lets the creator send their own finished videos from their own computer to their own TikTok account, using TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
